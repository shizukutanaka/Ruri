# Privacy

Ruri collects nothing.

- No accounts, no tracking, no analytics, no cookies, no telemetry.
- All tunings, chords, and settings stay on your device. Nothing is transmitted.
- No personally identifiable information (name, address, phone, IP, cookie identifiers) is collected or stored.
- Optional donations use an external Stripe-hosted page; payment data is handled entirely by Stripe and never reaches Ruri.
- The app works fully offline.

The strongest privacy guarantee is collecting nothing — that is the design.
